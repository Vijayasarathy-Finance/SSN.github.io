About me

I started my career in production support, keeping a .NET document-processing application running for US healthcare provider outreach. I was the person on the other end of P1 and P2 incidents, tuning SQL stored procedures and building the SSIS and SSRS pipelines that replaced manual reporting. In 2019 I moved into business analysis, and today I lead a team of 50+ as a Business Analyst and Team Lead.

That background shapes how I write requirements. I know what a database can and cannot do, where integrations tend to break and which edge cases only show up in real use, so my specifications are ones developers can build and testers can verify. It is also why I could rebuild the full specification for a legacy .NET and SQL system that had no documentation, and why that work enabled a React and Node.js migration.
